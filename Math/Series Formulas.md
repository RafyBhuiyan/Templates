# Series formulas

AUST Simplexity Team Notebook, section 6.5, page 24. Operators and convergence conditions are written explicitly.

| Series | Sum |
| --- | --- |
| Squares from 1 through n | `n(n + 1)(2n + 1) / 6` |
| Cubes from 1 through n | `[n(n + 1) / 2]^2` |
| First n arithmetic terms, starting at a with difference d | `n[2a + (n - 1)d] / 2` |
| First n geometric terms, starting at a with ratio r | `a(1 - r^n) / (1 - r)` for r ≠ 1, or `na` for r = 1 |
| Infinite geometric series, starting at a with ratio r | `a / (1 - r)` for −1 < r < 1 |
